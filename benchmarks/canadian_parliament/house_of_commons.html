An Act to change the names of certain electoral districts, 2026

The name of certain electoral districts in effect at the General Election of April 28, 2025, was changed by the enactment and the coming into force of Bill C-25, An Act to amend the Canada Elections Act and to enact An Act to change the names of certain electoral districts, 2026. The changes made to the names of these electoral districts came into force on September 16, 2026.
